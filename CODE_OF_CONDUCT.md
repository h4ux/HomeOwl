# Code of Conduct

HomeOwl follows the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**.

In short: be kind, be patient, assume good intent, and help keep the project welcoming for everyone — whatever their background or experience. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers privately through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/h4ux/HomeOwl/security/advisories/new) (it reaches the maintainers only) or by messaging a maintainer on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently ban contributors who break this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
